## Overview

Most streaming platforms and movie search engines rely on rigid genre tags, actor keywords, and algorithmic echo chambers. When users sit down to watch something, they don't think in metadata—they think in moods and vibes:

> *"A rainy-night neon-noir mystery with philosophical dialogue, similar to Blade Runner 2049, but more grounded and under two hours."*

Traditional search engines fail completely at this. **WatchMatch** is an agentic movie search engine that bridges the gap between natural human feelings and cinematic catalogs. It lets users search exactly how they speak, parsing nuanced vibes while giving them powerful granular filters to dial in the perfect watch.

---

## Key System Highlights & Architecture

### 1. Conversational Vibe & Intent Parser
- Employs an agentic LLM parsing layer that converts freeform queries into high-dimensional semantic attributes: visual aesthetic, emotional tone, narrative pacing, atmospheric intensity, and thematic subtext.
- Distinguishes subtle mood nuances—separating "dark and cynical" from "melancholic and contemplative."

### 2. Multi-Dimensional Filter Fusion
- Seamlessly bridges semantic matching with deterministic constraints:
  - **Temporal & Pacing:** Exact runtime windows, release decades, pacing tempo.
  - **Aesthetic & Tonal:** Neon-soaked, desaturated, gritty 90s, cozy acoustic, minimalist.
  - **Critical & Audience Metrics:** Custom thresholds across IMDb, Letterboxd, and Rotten Tomatoes scores.
  - **Availability:** Real-time OTT platform streaming availability and region filtering.

### 3. Agentic Rationale & Justification Engine
- For every recommendation, the engine doesn't just display a poster—it provides an agent-generated contextual breakdown explaining *why* it matches what you feel like watching, pinpointing specific scene vibes and thematic resonances.

### 4. High-Craft Discovery Interface
- Fast, tactile UI with interactive mood pills, instant trailer integration, and swipeable watch decks designed to eliminate decision paralysis in under 60 seconds.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 (App Router), React, TypeScript, Framer Motion, Tailwind CSS, Once UI Design Tokens.
- **Backend & APIs:** FastAPI, Python, TMDB API, Streaming Availability API.
- **AI & Reasoning:** Multi-agent semantic parser (Gemini API & local Ollama fallback), structured JSON schema validation.
