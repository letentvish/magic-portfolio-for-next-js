## Overview

Modern health tracking applications collect vast amounts of telemetry—sleep cycles, heart rate variability, energy levels, and food logs. However, they rarely provide synthesized understanding: raw metrics are isolated without accounting for seasonal changes, internal energy rhythms, or holistic balance.

**ShaktiVibe** connects fragmented biometric signals with Ayurvedic knowledge frameworks, translating raw telemetry into actionable, daily lifestyle guidance.

---

## Technical Architecture & Highlights

### 1. Multi-Modal Telemetry Ingestion
- Normalizes disparate health data (sleep duration, reported subjective energy, meal timings, stress indicators) into standardized temporal vectors without information loss.

### 2. Domain-Specific Ayurvedic RAG Engine
- Employs BM25 hybrid search over a curated Ayurvedic corpus to map acute states (e.g., elevated Vata/Pitta imbalance) against restorative dietary and lifestyle practices.
- Generates seasonal (*Ritucharya*) and daily (*Dinacharya*) adaptation plans tailored to individual physiological archetypes.

### 3. External Intelligence Bridge via MCP
- Features a local Model Context Protocol (MCP) server that allows external AI assistants to safely query health trends, retrieve dietary guidelines, and synchronize calendar recommendations.

---

## Design & Experience

- **Visualizing Vitality:** Designed with soft glassmorphic depth, dynamic circadian color transitions that shift with the day's solar progression, and accessible typography.
- **Actionable Micro-Rituals:** Instead of overwhelming health dashboards, users receive three high-impact daily micro-rituals grounded in both modern sleep science and ancient wellness principles.

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
- **Backend & Logic:** FastAPI, Python, SQLite, MCP Server.
- **AI/ML:** BM25 Hybrid Retrieval, RAG 2.0, Ollama local inference.
